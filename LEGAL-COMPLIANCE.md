# Legal and compliance gate

This repository must remain demo-credit only unless qualified counsel confirms that the proposed product may legally be operated in every target jurisdiction and all required approvals are active.

Before enabling money:

- Identify the licensed legal entity and permitted territories.
- Obtain written lottery/gaming analysis and required government permissions.
- Confirm whether a private operator may organize the draw or may only act for an authorized state operator.
- Obtain payment-provider and banking approval for the exact transaction flow.
- Implement age, identity, location, self-exclusion, deposit/participation limits, and responsible-play controls.
- Define KYC/AML, sanctions, suspicious-activity escalation, source-of-funds, and record-retention requirements.
- Confirm GST and withholding/tax treatment for ticket sales, prizes, refunds, and operator revenue.
- Publish approved rules, odds, draw schedule, cancellation/refund rules, complaint process, privacy policy, and terms.
- Arrange independent RNG/draw certification, security review, financial reconciliation, and periodic audit.

The product must fail closed: if licensing, location, age, identity, payment risk, or draw integrity cannot be verified, ticket purchase must be blocked.
